# Proposed first archived release — v0.1.0

**Repository:** https://github.com/alexodas/pythagorean-isotropy-lean

**Title:** The Coordinate Pythagorean Theorem from Planar Isotropy: An Axiomatic Derivation with Lie-Theoretic Extensions

## Contents

- **`PythagoreanIsotropy.lean`**: Lean 4/Mathlib proofs covering primitive planar rigidity, the algebraic derivation of the oriented quarter-turn, the resulting form of the linear rotation action, its infinitesimal generator under differentiability, and the coordinate Pythagorean theorem.
- **`manuscript/pythagorean_isotropy_ai_disclosure.pdf`**: revised and extended mathematical manuscript, including the group-theoretic proof and Lie-theoretic extension.
- **`manuscript/pythagorean_isotropy_ai_disclosure.tex`**: editable and reproducible LaTeX source.
- Pinned Lean toolchain, Mathlib dependency, and GitHub Actions checking both the formal proof and the PDF build.

## Provenance and attribution

The mathematical research request was initiated by **Alessandro Odasso**, who asked whether ChatGPT could find an original proof of the Pythagorean theorem. The original manuscript was **generated by ChatGPT, GPT-6 (High thinking effort)**. The Lean proof and later manuscript revisions were developed with AI assistance, with the proofs checked by Lean 4 and Mathlib via GitHub Actions.

The word *original* describes the prompt's objective and **does not certify historical or mathematical novelty**. The formalization proves the theorem conditional on its explicit geometric and group-theoretic assumptions, without taking a Euclidean Cartesian distance formula as the definition of primitive length.

## Limitations

The proof does not establish that its geometric assumptions are minimal or deducible from still weaker axioms, and it does not independently establish priority over all earlier proofs.
